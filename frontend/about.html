About Maddy

Maddy

Owner - Lead Tutor

Hello! My name is Maddy, dedicated music instructor with a Bachelor of Arts in Popular Music and Worship Arts from Catawba College. My experience extends beyond the classroom, having performed my own shows, managed artists, and worked as a songwriter and music producer. Since 2020, I have been teaching private lessons, guiding over 24 students on their musical journeys. I'm excited to bring my practical experience and passion for music to your lessons!
